Juseong Park

Senior Engineer @ Qualcomm Wireless R&D | Ph.D. in ECE, UT Austin

I am a wireless communications researcher at Qualcomm. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Jeffrey G. Andrews and was a member of the Wireless Networking and Communications Group (WNCG) and the 6G@UT Research Center.

My research interests lie at the intersection of wireless communications, signal processing, and machine learning, with a particular focus on multi-user MIMO, CSI acquisition and feedback, channel modeling, and generative learning for wireless systems.

Prior to joining Qualcomm, I received my B.E. and M.E. degrees from Korea University, advised by Prof. Inkyu Lee, and completed research internships at Nokia Bell Labs, Nokia Standards, and Qualcomm.

Research Interests

  • Machine Learning for Wireless: Generative Models, Deep Learning, Representation Learning
  • Signal Processing: Optimization, Compressive Sensing
